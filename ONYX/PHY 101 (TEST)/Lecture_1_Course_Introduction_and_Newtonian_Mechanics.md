- **Classical vs. Quantum Regimes:** Classical Newtonian mechanics (see [[Lecture_1_Course_Introduction_and_Newtonian_Mechanics]]) treats particles as having well‑defined trajectories, whereas quantum mechanics imposes intrinsic uncertainties.
